# Refunds and freshness

## Our freshness guarantee

If an arrangement arrives wilted, damaged or noticeably different from the
photo on the website, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We
replace the arrangement or refund the full order price, including the delivery
fee. You do not need to send the arrangement back.

## How long a refund takes

Card refunds are requested the same working day we approve them. Your bank
usually posts the money in 3 to 5 working days, and some banks take up to 10.
Refunds to a PayPal balance can take up to 30 days to show.

## Late deliveries

If a guaranteed next-day delivery arrives later than the paid-for window, we
refund the delivery fee on request. The standard delivery fee is not refundable
when the parcel arrives within the estimated window shown at checkout.

## What we cannot refund

We cannot refund an order for a change of mind once the flowers are cut and
arranged, because every bouquet is made to order. Subscriptions are covered by
the rules in Accounts and subscriptions.
