# Delivery and shipping

## Delivery windows

Standard delivery arrives between 8 am and 6 pm. Next-day delivery must be
ordered before 2 pm. Guaranteed morning delivery arrives before noon and
costs extra.

## Areas we cover

We deliver within a 25 mile radius of our workshop in Fernway. Postcodes
outside the radius are shown as unavailable at checkout. We do not deliver
outside the United Kingdom.

## Sundays and bank holidays

We deliver on Sundays inside the covered radius for a Sunday supplement.
There are no deliveries on bank holidays; orders for those dates move to the
next working day.

## When nobody answers

If nobody answers, our driver leaves the parcel in a safe place or with a
neighbour and photographs it. If neither is possible, the parcel returns to
the workshop and we contact you the same evening. A redelivery can be booked
for the standard fee.

## Changing an address

You can change the delivery address or the card message up to 4 pm the
working day before delivery by replying to your order confirmation email.
After that the route is printed and the address cannot be changed.
