# Returns and substitutions

## Made to order

Every bouquet is cut and arranged after you order, so we do not accept
returns. If something is wrong with the arrangement, the freshness guarantee
in Refunds and freshness applies instead.

## Substitutions

When a stem is out of season, our florists substitute a flower of equal or
greater value in a similar colour. Substitutions are listed on the delivery
note. If you do not accept substitutions, tick "no substitutions" at checkout
and we will contact you before substituting anything.

## Vases and packaging

Vases and gift packaging can be swapped before the arrangement is made if you
contact us before 6 pm the day before delivery. Vases that arrive broken are
replaced free of charge; send a photo within 48 hours.
