# Accounts and subscriptions

## Subscription plans

Weekly, fortnightly and monthly plans renew automatically. You can pause,
skip or cancel a plan from the account page at any time before the renewal
date. Cancelling after renewal stops the next delivery but does not refund
the one already paid for.

## Account deletion

Deleting an account removes stored addresses and payment methods. Order
records stay for 6 years because tax law requires it. Ask support to delete
them sooner if your local law gives you the right.

## Gift messages and preferences

Saved preferences (allergies, colours to avoid, no substitutions) apply to
every order on the account until you change them.
