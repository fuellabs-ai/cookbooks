SUPPORT HOURS AND CONTACT

PHONE

Support answers calls from 8 am to 8 pm on working days. Outside those hours
the line plays a message and asks you to email.

EMAIL

Emails sent before 6 pm on a working day get a reply the same day. Emails
sent on a weekend or bank holiday get a reply on the next working day.

POST

not accepted back.
