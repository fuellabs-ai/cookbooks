# Sympathy and bereavement orders

## Urgent sympathy deliveries

Sympathy orders placed before 2 pm can be delivered the same day inside the
covered radius. Write "sympathy" in the order note and we move the order to
the front of the route.

## Funeral timing

If the funeral time moves after you order, tell us at least 6 hours before
the delivery window and we move the delivery time at no charge.

## Documentation for refunds

For a refund on a sympathy order that could not be delivered on time, we ask
for the funeral home name and the service time. A photo of the arrangement is
not needed when the delivery was refused or the venue closed. Refunds follow
the same 3 to 5 working day timeline as in Refunds and freshness.

## Standing remembrance orders

Weekly or monthly remembrance arrangements for a grave or memorial can be
scheduled for up to 12 months. Each delivery is billed separately and any
delivery can be paused from the account page.
