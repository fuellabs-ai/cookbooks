# SaaS diligence answers

Quillix answers from a SaaS procurement diligence form, November 2025.

## Reports

A SOC 2 Type II report covering security and availability is available under
NDA. The report is issued annually.

## Sub-processors

The current sub-processor list is published on the trust page and customers
are emailed 30 days before a new sub-processor goes live.

## Incident response

The on-call rotation runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. A named incident
manager is assigned within 1 hour of a confirmed severity 1 incident.
