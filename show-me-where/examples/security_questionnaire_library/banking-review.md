# Banking customer review

Quillix answers from a banking customer security review, September 2025.

## Access control

The admin console supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect for single sign-on.
Role templates are read-only, operator and administrator. Sessions expire
after 30 minutes of inactivity.

## Testing

We run an annual third-party penetration test and share the executive
summary under NDA. Customers may run their own test against a staging
environment with 10 working days notice.

## Backups

Backups run nightly and are encrypted with AES-256. Backup copies are kept
for 30 days, then destroyed.
