# Hospital IT review

Quillix answers from a hospital IT customer review, January 2026.

## Staff screening

Employees with access to customer data pass a background check and sign a
confidentiality agreement every two years. Contractors work under the same
terms.

## Uptime

The service carries a 99.9 percent monthly uptime commitment on the standard
plan. Planned maintenance is announced 5 working days ahead and stays inside
the Sunday 2 am to 5 am window.

## Data removal

When an account is cancelled, production data is deleted within 30 days and
backups age out on their 30 day schedule. We confirm deletion in writing on
request.
