# Housing help

## Homelessness support

If you are at risk of homelessness within 56 days, contact the housing
options team for a prevention plan. Emergency same-day assessment is
available in office hours for families with children.

## Private renting problems

For disrepair, report to environmental health; we inspect and can serve the
landlord a notice. Deposits not protected within 30 days can also be
reported to us.

## Council house repairs

Report repairs on the tenant portal. Emergency repairs (no heating, a leak
you cannot stop, no lock on an outside door) are attended within 24 hours;
routine repairs within 20 working days.

## Applying for a council house

Join the housing register online. Banding depends on need; you must live or
work in the district and not owe rent arrears over 2 weeks on any previous
tenancy.
