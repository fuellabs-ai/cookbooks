# Parking permits

## Resident permits

Residents of zones A to E can buy up to 2 resident permits per household a
year at 40 pounds each. Zone B permits also cover the Grosvenor Street bays.

## Visitor permits

Each household can buy 20 day visitor permits a year at 2 pounds each.
Visitor permits are digital; give the visitor's plate to the warden line
before they arrive.

## Where permits apply

Permits cover bays marked with your zone letter on signs. They do not cover
pay-and-display bays, the leisure centre car park or loading bays between
11 am and 1 pm.

## Damaged and lost permits

Lost physical permits are replaced once a year free of charge; a second
replacement costs 10 pounds. Report a changed car within 14 days or the
permit is void for the new plate.
