Household waste recycling centre

What you can bring

Household recycling, wood, metal, garden waste, small electricals, car batteries, and engine oil in a sealed container. Bring proof of address such as a council tax bill or a driving licence.

What we do not accept

Tradesman's waste and business waste are not accepted, even for a charge.

Trailers and vans

Trailers up to 1.8 m long are allowed at weekends only.

Opening hours

The site is open 9 am to 5 pm, seven days a week. Last entry is 10 minutes before closing.

Asbestos

Asbestos must be pre-booked 48 hours ahead and handed to staff double-bagged.
