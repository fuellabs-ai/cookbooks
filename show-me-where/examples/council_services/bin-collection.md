# Bin collection

## Collection days

Green (recycling) bins are collected every Tuesday, black (general waste)
bins every Tuesday, and brown (garden waste) bins on alternate Tuesdays from
March to November. Bins must be out by 7 am with the handles towards the road.

## Missed collections

Report a missed bin within 2 working days on the council website or by phone.
Crews return within 3 working days of a confirmed miss. A bin put out late,
blocked by a parked car or containing the wrong materials is not counted as
missed.

## What goes in each bin

Green bins take paper, card, cans, glass bottles and plastic bottles. Black
bins take everything else that is not hazardous. Brown bins take grass,
leaves and branches up to 15 cm thick. No soil, no food and no plastic bags
in brown bins.

## Bulky item collection

Households get 3 free bulky item collections a year, up to 4 items each. Book
online and items are collected on your normal collection day. Mattresses need
a separate mattress cover, sold at the depot for 4 pounds.
