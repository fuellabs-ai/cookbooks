# Council tax

## Bands and how to pay

Your band letter is on your bill. Pay over 10 months from April to January,
or over 12 months by direct debit. Missed instalments trigger a reminder; two
reminders in one year make the full balance due.

## Discounts

A 25 percent discount applies if you are the only adult in the home.
Full-time students and live-in carers are ignored when counting adults.
Second homes pay a 100 percent premium, and empty homes pay the premium after
2 years.

## Help if you fall behind

Contact the revenues team before the next instalment date. We can spread
arrears over the rest of the year or set a direct debit you can afford.
Discretionary reductions exist for hardship; apply on the website with
recent payslips.

## Changes you must report

Tell us within 21 days if someone moves in or out, the property becomes
empty, or you start working from home as a business.
