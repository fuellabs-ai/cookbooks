# Remote work

## Working from home

Ravenswood is remote-first. There is no attendance target; teams agree their
own overlap hours inside the 10 am to 4 pm UK core window.

## Home equipment

Every home desk gets a laptop, a monitor and a keyboard and mouse. There is
a one-off chair and desk allowance of 200 pounds, claimed through expenses.
Home internet is paid at 25 pounds a month.

## Working from another country

You can work from another country for up to 20 working days a year with
manager and HR approval at least 4 weeks ahead. Countries on the payroll
blocked list cannot be approved.

## Co-working

A co-working membership near your home can be approved for roles that need
it. Ask HR before signing anything; invoices go to the company, not through
expenses.
