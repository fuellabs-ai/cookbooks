# Notice period

## Probation

New joiners have a 6 month probation with a review at 3 months and 6 months.
Either side can end employment on 1 week notice during probation.

## Notice after probation

Notice after probation depends on service: under 2 years is 4 weeks, 2 to 5
years is 8 weeks, and over 5 years is 12 weeks.

## Pay in lieu and garden leave

The company can pay your notice period in lieu instead of keeping you
working, or place you on garden leave. Benefits continue through a garden
leave period.

## Resignation

Hand resignations to your manager in writing and copy HR. Your last day is
counted from the date the notice is accepted.
