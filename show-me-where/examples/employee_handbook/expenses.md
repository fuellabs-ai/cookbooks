# Expenses

## Receipts

Attach a receipt to every expense over 10 pounds. Below that, a note of what,
when and why is enough. Submit expenses within 60 days of the purchase.

## Travel and meals

Mileage is paid at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles a year. When you
travel for work, meals are covered up to 35 pounds a day. Hotels are booked
through the travel desk, never self-paid.

## Client entertainment

Client meals and hospitality up to 150 pounds need your manager's approval
before you spend. Above that, add a note on who attended and why.

## What is not claimable

Alcohol outside a client meal, traffic fines, and costs already covered by
an allowance, such as the home internet payment, cannot be claimed.
