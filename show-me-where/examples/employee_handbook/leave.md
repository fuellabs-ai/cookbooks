# Leave

## Annual leave

Full-time staff get 25 days of annual leave a year plus public holidays.
Part-time staff get the same total, pro-rated across their days. Book leave
in the HR portal; long breaks of a week or more need 4 weeks notice.

## Carrying leave over

Up to 5 unused days carry into the next leave year and must be used by
31 March. Anything left after that is lost unless your manager approves an
exception in writing.

## Sick pay

Company sick pay is 10 days a year on full pay, then Statutory Sick Pay.
You must tell your manager before 10 am on the first day and confirm with a
doctor's note after 7 calendar days.

## Parental leave

A primary carer gets 26 weeks on full pay. A secondary carer gets 4 weeks on
full pay, taken within 6 months of the birth or placement. Both start with a
conversation with HR in the second trimester or after approval to adopt.

## Unpaid leave

Requests for unpaid leave go to your manager and HR. Anything over 10
working days needs a written reason and a return date.
